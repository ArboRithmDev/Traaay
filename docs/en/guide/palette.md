# Floating Top Traaay Palette

The **Top Traaay Floating Palette** embodies **Strata 3** (Soft Mint `#34D399`): bringing seamless, instantaneous access regardless of menu bar state or display clutter.

---

## Why a Floating Palette?

When menu bars are congested or items disappear behind the physical notch, reaching for them in the standard status bar becomes clunky and unreliable.

The compact **Top Traaay** strip floats unobtrusively near the top edge of your screen:
- It aggregates **all running utilities**, whether visible, folded, or occluded by the notch.
- It displays **amber warning dots** on items currently hidden behind the notch.
- It provides a generous, precise click surface that eliminates accidental adjacent mis-clicks.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Application-Based Grouping
Icons belonging to the same suite or process are visually grouped into cohesive capsules, speeding up visual identification.

### 2. Cursor Teleportation & Native Click
Clicking an icon in the Top Traaay palette triggers instant cursor teleportation to the exact status item position in the menu bar, opening its native menu on the spot.
- If the item is **hidden under the notch**, Traaay safely prevents blind clicks and issues a notification instead (see [Gestures & Shortcuts](./shortcuts)).

### 3. Direct App Launch via ⌘+Click
Want to open the full application window instead of its small status menu?
Hold **⌘ (Command)** while clicking any item in the palette to activate the parent application directly without moving your mouse pointer.
