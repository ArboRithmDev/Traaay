# Visual Identity & Design

The Traaay brand identity is built around **The 3 Strata Architecture**, reflected in the 3 "a" characters of its name: **Tr<span class="traaay-a1">a</span><span class="traaay-a2">a</span><span class="traaay-a3">a</span>y**.

---

## Philosophy: The Three Strata

Each stratum embodies a distinct level of control with its own signature color:

| Stratum | Name | Hex Code | Purpose & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stratum 1** | **Glacier Cyan** | `#38BDF8` | **Clarity & Separation**: The collapsible separator reducing mental clutter. |
| **Stratum 2** | **Electric Blue** | `#3B82F6` | **Control & Awareness**: Notch detection and screen space mastery. |
| **Stratum 3** | **Soft Mint** | `#34D399` | **Fluidity & Access**: The floating palette and instant zero-friction interactions. |

The entire visual system is grounded on a deep dark base, **Dark Obsidian** (`#0F1117`), providing optimal contrast and natural synergy with macOS Dark Mode.

---

## The Wordmark

The textual logo is drawn as vector outlines from Inter, tightened by `-0.03em`. The three « a » carry the colours of the three strata; « Tr » and « y » take on the colour of the surrounding text — light on a dark ground, dark on a light one.

```
Tr [a : Cyan] [a : Blue] [a : Mint] y
```

## macOS Application Icon

The Traaay app icon uses the official Apple squircle canvas with the following craftsmanship:
- **Background**: Vertical Dark Obsidian gradient (`#1A1D27` to `#090A0E`) with an upper metallic rim.
- **Core Symbol**: Three cascading horizontal rounded capsules with vibrant gradients and subtle glassy reflections.
- **Drop Shadow**: Native dual-layer elevation aligned with macOS Sequoia / Sonoma standards.
